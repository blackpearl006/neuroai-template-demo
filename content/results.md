### Where the signal lives

Saliency concentrates in association cortex, lateral prefrontal cortex and a
handful of subcortical structures, with the inferior parietal lobule and caudate
among the single most influential regions. Twenty-one regions account for more
than half of the total attribution, and that core set is stable across atlases —
strong evidence the model reads anatomy rather than a parcellation-specific quirk.

### What it means

A compact convolutional model recovers a reproducible reasoning signal from raw
structural MRI, and its predictions are driven by anatomically plausible regions.
That combination — predictive *and* interpretable — is what makes anatomy-based
cognitive prediction usable as a marker rather than a black box.
