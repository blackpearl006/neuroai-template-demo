### Where the signal lives

Saliency concentrates in association cortex, medial temporal lobe and a handful
of subcortical structures, with the lateral ventricles and hippocampus among the
single most influential regions. Eighteen regions account for more than half of
the total attribution, and that core set is stable across atlases — strong
evidence the model reads anatomy rather than a parcellation-specific quirk.

### What it means

A compact convolutional model recovers a robust brain-age signal from raw
structural MRI, and its predictions are driven by anatomically plausible regions.
That combination — accurate *and* interpretable — is what makes brain age usable
as a marker rather than a black box.
